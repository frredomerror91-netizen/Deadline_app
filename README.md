# Deadline Desk

Deadline Desk 是一个 Windows 桌面截止时间管理工具。把作业、项目和待办放进清单，再用桌面悬浮窗随时查看还有多久到期。

Deadline Desk helps you keep track of deadlines on Windows. Add assignments, projects, and other tasks to a list, and keep an eye on the time left in a small floating window.

[下载最新版 / Download the latest version](https://github.com/frredomerror91-netizen/Deadline_app/releases/latest)

## 功能 / Features

- **任务清单**：添加或修改任务名称和截止时间，按今天、本周或月份查看。
  **Task lists:** Add or edit task names and deadlines, and browse tasks by day, week, or month.
- **悬浮倒计时**：在桌面上查看任务和剩余时间，可以拖动、缩放、锁定位置，也可以贴边收起。
  **Floating countdown:** Keep tasks and the time remaining on your desktop. Move or resize the window, lock it in place, or tuck it away at the screen edge.
- **截止提醒颜色**：用不同颜色标出临近截止、逾期和已完成的任务。
  **Deadline colors:** Spot approaching deadlines, overdue tasks, and completed items at a glance.
- **完成清单与回收站**：查看已完成的任务，找回误删的内容。
  **Completed tasks and recycle bin:** Review finished tasks and restore items deleted by mistake.
- **主题与语言**：提供浅色、灰色、深色主题，以及中文、英文和日文界面。
  **Themes and languages:** Choose a light, gray, or dark theme, with Chinese, English, and Japanese interfaces.
- **本地保存**：任务和设置保存在自己的电脑上。
  **Local storage:** Your tasks and preferences stay on your computer.
